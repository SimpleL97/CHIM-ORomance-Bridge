CHIM ORomance Bridge - changelog
================================

1.0.0
- First release.
- AI actions through ORomance Plus: Feel_Affection, Feel_Hurt, Become_Partner,
  Marry_Player (several spouses), Take_Household_Duty, Give_Homemade_Meal.
- The Amulet of Mara proposal line back in the normal dialogue menu.
- Every OStim scene with the player raises ORomance like and love (MCM amounts,
  ORomance's own ceilings; aggressive scenes do not count).
- Agents know their ORomance feelings; spouses know their marriage and each other,
  talk to each other, share household duties and ask for the marital duty.
- Wanting children (MCM names); optional Fertility Mode 3 support: cycle and
  pregnancy awareness, pregnancies from OStim Standalone scenes.
- News of pregnancies and births (Fertility Mode): the AI agents around hear of them,
  and the other spouses and partners know who is expecting (MCM).
- The ones who love the player know whom the player lately got engaged to or married;
  spouses know about a new spouse.
- Works with CHIM OStim Bridge and CHIM Vampire Bridge; none of them needs another.
- With ORomance's odds on, the agent knows beforehand whether ORomance would accept a
  couple or a marriage right now, and says "not yet" in character instead of saying yes
  and then being turned down (ORomance wants a couple first, and enough love).
- The proposal line in the dialogue menu is hidden while the two are engaged and
  waiting for the wedding at the Temple of Mara.
- Fix: a refused action ("there is a fight going on", "she is not here") is answered at
  once, in character, with the reason. Newer CHIM versions no longer pass an action's
  result to the mod, so the agent only learned of it in the next reply. Successful
  actions get no extra reply; every refusal is also written to the server log.
- Notifications and a log file in the game and on the CHIM server (MCM: Debug).
- MCM through MCM Helper in 12 languages; the server plugin installs itself.
