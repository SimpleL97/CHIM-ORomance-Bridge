CHIM ORomance Bridge - changelog
================================

1.0.0
- First release.
- AI actions through ORomance Plus: Feel_Affection, Feel_Hurt, Become_Partner,
  Marry_Player (several spouses), Take_Household_Duty, Give_Homemade_Meal.
- The Amulet of Mara proposal line back in the normal dialogue menu.
- Every OStim scene with the player raises ORomance like and love (MCM amounts,
  ORomance's own ceilings; aggressive scenes do not count).
- Agents know their ORomance feelings; spouses know their marriage and each other,
  talk to each other, share household duties and ask for the marital duty.
- Wanting children (MCM names); optional Fertility Mode 3 support: cycle and
  pregnancy awareness, pregnancies from OStim Standalone scenes.
- News of pregnancies and births (Fertility Mode): the AI agents around hear of them,
  and the other spouses and partners know who is expecting (MCM).
- The ones who love the player know whom the player lately got engaged to or married;
  spouses know about a new spouse.
- Works with CHIM OStim Bridge and CHIM Vampire Bridge; none of them needs another.
- With ORomance's odds on, the agent knows beforehand whether ORomance would accept a
  couple or a marriage right now, and says "not yet" in character instead of saying yes
  and then being turned down (ORomance wants a couple first, and enough love).
- An engagement is kept apart from a marriage: the fiancee knows the wedding is waiting at
  the Temple of Mara (Maramal) and is not treated as a wife before it.
- The proposal line in the dialogue menu is hidden while the two are engaged and
  waiting for the wedding at the Temple of Mara.
- The vanilla wedding no longer gets stuck: a follower with a voice of their own has no
  vanilla acceptance line, so the Temple of Mara never learnt of the engagement and
  Maramal offered no ceremony. The bridge now does what that line would have done
  (also for an engagement already stuck in a save).
- MCM "Partners do not get jealous" (off by default): ORomance Plus breaks up with you
  when a partner or spouse sees you in a scene with someone else, whatever their
  character says about sharing. On, their monogamy desire is kept below ORomance's
  jealousy line; off, ORomance's own values come back.
- MCM "Spouse stays a follower after the wedding" (Spouses, on by default). The wedding
  itself (vanilla and ORomance Plus alike) dismisses a bride or groom who is your follower,
  holds them at the temple repeating a line of the ceremony with no dialogue menu until you
  have left for hours, then sends them to "their home". Now whoever followed you when you
  proposed follows you again the moment you are married (also with Nether's Follower
  Framework), and the wedding lets them go right after the ceremony.
- A second (third, ...) spouse gets ORomance Plus's own wedding at the Temple of Mara too,
  as in ORomance itself, and it no longer gets stuck for a follower with a voice of their
  own (the same missing acceptance line as with the first wedding). The agent knows it is
  an engagement until Maramal marries you; a proposal while another wedding is waiting
  names who is waiting.
- Making up: after ORomance breaks up with you (a quarrel, being caught cheating) the NPC
  turns hostile, will not talk ("I don't like you") and runs off, and ORomance's own
  apology always fails. Now the agent knows it and, when you truly apologise, can forgive
  you in character (Feel_Affection "forgive"): dislike and hate go down and they are
  friendly again. A partnership is not given back by itself.
- Your children (MCM, Children): Fertility Mode births whose father is you are kept as
  your children - mother, age, name once Fertility Mode gives one. The mother talks about
  your child, everyone close to you knows the children and whose they are; the news of a
  birth names the father.
- Move_In (MCM, Spouses): a spouse of ORomance Plus's own weddings moves into the home you
  agree on in conversation, and knows it is theirs.
- Dates and anniversaries (MCM, Spouses): an evening at an inn with a spouse or partner is
  a date. They know when the last one was, ask you out when it was long ago (MCM days),
  and spouses know the monthly and yearly anniversaries of the wedding.
- Fix: a refused action ("there is a fight going on", "she is not here") is answered at
  once, in character, with the reason. Newer CHIM versions no longer pass an action's
  result to the mod, so the agent only learned of it in the next reply. Successful
  actions get no extra reply; every refusal is also written to the server log.
- Notifications and a log file in the game and on the CHIM server (MCM: Debug).
- MCM through MCM Helper in 12 languages; the server plugin installs itself.
