CHIM ORomance Bridge - changelog
================================

1.0.0
- First release.
- AI actions through ORomance Plus: Feel_Affection, Feel_Hurt, Become_Partner,
  Marry_Player (several spouses), Take_Household_Duty, Give_Homemade_Meal.
- The Amulet of Mara proposal line back in the normal dialogue menu.
- Every OStim scene with the player raises ORomance like and love (MCM amounts,
  ORomance's own ceilings; aggressive scenes do not count).
- Agents know their ORomance feelings; spouses know their marriage and each other,
  talk to each other, share household duties and ask for the marital duty.
- Wanting children (MCM names); optional Fertility Mode 3 support: cycle and
  pregnancy awareness, pregnancies from OStim Standalone scenes.
- MCM through MCM Helper in 12 languages; the server plugin installs itself.
